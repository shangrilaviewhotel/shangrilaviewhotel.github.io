CITY LOCKDOWN: An Upcoming Entertainment Experience in Port Harcourt

Saturday, 7 November 2026 · 5:00 PM · Shangri-La View Hotel

Port Harcourt is preparing for an exciting entertainment experience as CITY LOCKDOWN arrives at Shangri-La View Hotel on Saturday, 7 November 2026. Built around dance, music, comedy, networking and entertainment, the event brings together DJs, hypemen, artists and dancers for an evening of shared experiences.

Meet the line-up

The announced DJs are DJ KizzyBlinkz and DJ D Flex. The hypemen are Hypeman Charlimental and Hypeman Richlife. Artists include Skyrichttt, Skidoski and Graceboi, with dance performances from African Micheal Jackson and Successful Dancers.

Why attend CITY LOCKDOWN?

  1. Enjoy an evening of music and DJ performances.
  2. Experience the announced live artist line-up.
  3. Watch dance and entertainment showcases.
  4. Enjoy comedy, hype and audience interaction.
  5. Meet friends and make new connections.
  6. Discover Shangri-La View Hotel in Port Harcourt.

Discover the venue

Shangri-La View Hotel is located on Airport Road, when coming from the Obiri-Ikwerre Flyover axis, after Dunamis Church and before Dustbin, Port Harcourt. Guests who need directions or event information can contact the event team directly.

Plan your visit

CITY LOCKDOWN takes place on Saturday, 7 November 2026, beginning at 5:00 PM. For attendance enquiries, VIP table information and directions, call or WhatsApp 08110831894. Guests interested in accommodation can also check room availability through the hotel's booking page.
