Our Story

Hospitality,
thoughtfully delivered.

Shangri-La View Hotel stands as a destination where comfort, elegance and genuine hospitality come together. Located in Rumuosi Victory Estate along Airport Road, Port Harcourt, the hotel provides a welcoming environment for guests seeking quality accommodation, relaxation, business convenience and memorable experiences.

01 · The Beginning

Built around
the guest.

Shangri-La View Hotel was established with a simple but meaningful purpose: to create a hotel experience where guests can feel comfortable, respected and genuinely welcomed.

From accommodation and hospitality to our leisure spaces and guest services, every part of the experience is designed with the guest in mind.
